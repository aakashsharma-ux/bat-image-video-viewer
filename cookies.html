Cookie policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Short version: Bat-Viewer sets no cookies of any kind. It can keep two small items in your browser’s local storage, and the one that is optional (your theme) is saved only after you press “Allow”.

1. Cookies

Bat-Viewer does not set first-party or third-party cookies: no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no social-media cookies and no login cookies.

2. Local storage

Local storage is a small area of your own browser that a site can write to. It is like a cookie in that it persists, but unlike a cookie it is never sent to a server with your requests. Bat-Viewer uses it like this:

NamePurposeNeeds your consent?How long
bv-themeRemembers whether you chose the Night or the Moon themeYes. Saved only after you press “Allow”Until you clear it (see below)
bv-consentRemembers your answer to the banner so it does not ask on every visitNo. It records your choice and nothing elseUntil you clear it (see below)

Nothing else is written to cookies, local storage, session storage or any other browser database. Your links, notes, edits and the Common Notes text exist only in the page’s memory and are gone when you reload.

3. Your choice

  • The banner offers “No thanks” and “Allow” with equal prominence. Bat-Viewer works the same either way; with “No thanks” the theme simply resets to Night when you reload.
  • To change your answer, use Privacy settings in the footer of the app.
  • To remove both items, use Delete my data in the footer of the app, or clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings.

4. Content from other sites

The pictures and videos you load come straight from the sites that host them. Those sites may use cookies or similar technology when your browser fetches their files. Bat-Viewer does not control that; see the privacy information of the site you are loading from. Your browser’s own settings let you block third-party cookies.

5. More information

See the privacy policy for everything Bat-Viewer does with data, and the terms of service for the rules of use.
